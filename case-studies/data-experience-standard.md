# LVIS — portfolio-wide data experience standard
Approved direction: data design is central, not decorative. All demos label synthetic data; public statistics require source URL, indicator, geography, period, unit, retrieval date and caveats. Never treat country examples as national benchmarks without evidence.
## WorkAtlas
Synthetic distributed workspaces across Asia, Europe, Americas and Africa; anonymous teams and roles. Interactive workload matrix (counts by status), bottleneck timeline (elapsed hours), ownership flow (task counts), reassignment simulation with permissions preserved. Context source: https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/ (working time; not company performance).
## Signal
Preserve existing NOVA evidence classifications. Add synthetic regional scenario filters, annotated evidence comparison and sensitivity waterfall with transparent assumptions, units and denominators. Context source: https://data.unesco.org/explore/assets/wdi001/ (World Development Indicators; select relevant indicators only). Never combine incompatible samples.
## Field Guide
Synthetic exhibition layouts across global cities, explicitly fictional venues. Interactive route diagram, time budget, accessibility constraints and alternate stops; travel/dwell minutes distinguished. Actual museum datasets require individual source verification and reuse rights; no invented attendance claims.
## Second Nature
Interactive capacity sculpture, alternative comparison, budget/time trade-offs and reversible choices. Context source: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/time-use-database.html (30 OECD countries; survey years and methods vary, not global coverage). Personal demo values are synthetic or user entered; no normative wellness scores.
## Shared interaction and QA
Progressive disclosure, keyboard controls, accessible text equivalents, reduced motion, mobile layouts, no color-only encoding. Every chart must answer a decision question; chart and text update from the same state. Check totals, units, missing values, bounds, sample comparability, source links and reset. Test at 390 and 1440 px. Maintain existing prototype routes and approved aesthetic. No release until tests and review pass.
